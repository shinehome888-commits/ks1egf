About Us

KS1 Empire Global Foundation (KS1EGF) is a registered nonprofit organization founded on a simple belief: technology must serve humanity with transparency, ethics, and accessibility at its core.

We exist to bridge the digital divide by delivering secure tools, digital literacy programs, and community-centered learning platforms to underserved populations across Africa and the Global South.

Unlike corporate tech models, we build open, ethical, and community-governed systems that prioritize human dignity, lifelong learning, and sustainable progress.

Our Mission

We believe technology should empower—not exclude. Our mission is to build and deploy secure digital tools rooted in three core principles:

1. Access
Digital tools and learning resources must be available to all—regardless of income, connectivity, or location.

2. Ownership
Communities must control their data, identity, and digital footprint.

3. Empowerment
Technology should foster critical thinking, self-reliance, and community resilience.

Through ethical innovation and digital literacy, we are building a future where technology serves as a bridge to opportunity and dignity for all.
